About

What is ZKA Labs?

A technology laboratory building AI agents, APIs, automation systems, and next-generation infrastructure.

🎯

Mission

Build organized, scalable AI infrastructure that grows into hundreds of integrated projects without changing the foundation.

🔬

Focus Areas

AI Agents, API platforms, workflow automation, persistent memory systems, research experiments, and SaaS infrastructure.

⚡

Philosophy

Premium over fast. Scalable over simple. Every decision optimizes for long-term maintainability and professional quality.

🌐

Infrastructure

Production-grade systems designed for reliability, performance, and seamless integration across the ecosystem.

🧠

Research
