About

What is ZKA Labs?

ZKA Labs is a technology laboratory focused on building AI agents, APIs, automation systems, and next-generation infrastructure. Every project is designed to scale from prototype to production.

🎯

Mission

Build organized, scalable, and professional AI infrastructure that can grow into hundreds of integrated projects without changing the foundation.

🔬

Focus Areas

AI Agents, API platforms, workflow automation, persistent memory systems, research experiments, and SaaS infrastructure.

⚡

Philosophy

Premium over fast. Scalable over simple. Every decision optimizes for long-term maintainability and professional quality.

🌐

Open Source

Built in public. Every repository, every tool, every experiment is open for anyone to learn from, use, and contribute to.
