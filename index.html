About

What is ZKA Labs?

A small but focused technology lab organizing AI agents, automation, APIs, and infrastructure into one durable operating system.

🎯

Mission

Build a foundation that can grow from one agent into a full ecosystem without becoming messy, fragile, or dependent on hype.

🔬

Focus Areas

AI Agents, API platforms, workflow automation, persistent memory systems, research experiments, and SaaS infrastructure.

⚡

Philosophy

Clear systems over noise. Useful infrastructure over demos. Every layer should be understandable, reusable, and hard to break.

🌐

Infrastructure

Production-grade systems designed for reliability, performance, and seamless integration across the ecosystem.

🧠

Research

AI safety, alignment, experimental models, and pushing the boundaries of what autonomous agents can achieve.

🔓

Open Source

Built in public where it makes sense: repositories, experiments, notes, and tools that others can inspect, learn from, and reuse.
